Coach's notes

How to start lifting

Start with 2 to 3 full-body sessions a week. Learn the squat, hinge, push, pull and carry with light weight, then add a little load each week.

Protein basics

Spread protein across your meals and pair it with strength training. Needs vary by person, so ask a doctor or dietitian if you have a health condition.

Rest is training

Muscle grows while you recover. Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep and at least one rest day a week.
